About

A senior software engineer whose work has followed one thread: build things people actually use.

Today I'm at ReelMetrics in Leiden, building the big-data analytics platform behind the global gaming industry. It's a long way from where I started — years spent shipping mobile and web products across African markets, at the intersection of good engineering and genuine usefulness: language learning, civic accountability, literacy, and finance.

I'm passionate about solving problems, developing people, and applying lean and agile principles to daily work. AWS- and Kubernetes-certified, I work across Rails, Java/Android, PostgreSQL and cloud infrastructure. When I'm not shipping, I'm writing about the craft — and the messy human side of building products.
